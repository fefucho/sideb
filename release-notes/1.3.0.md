# Side B 1.3.0

A faster browsing and discovery experience, with the latest playback and fullscreen improvements together on macOS and Windows. Side B remains in beta.

## Search and discovery

- Provider search results retain their original sections, filters, primary result and related items, with accurate playback and navigation actions.
- More compact search layouts, recycled result viewports and floating suggestions that keep the page in place.
- Retained pages preserve their content when opening search overlays and suspend unnecessary background work.
- Fullscreen Related reuses visible shelves and song columns, preserves scroll position and handles refresh, track changes and account changes correctly.

## Playback and presentation

- A more flexible player bar with room for metadata, scrolling long titles, linked artist credits and consistent actions.
- Explicit and clean indicators use confirmed provider metadata; missing information is kept unknown.
- Keeps the word-synced lyrics, animated artwork and playback improvements from the Lyrics Update.
- Windows includes mouse navigation controls for horizontal content and locally bundled Inter typography.

## Downloads

- **macOS:** `SideB-macOS.zip` — Apple Silicon, macOS 15 or later; ad-hoc signed, not notarized.
- **Windows installer:** `SideB-Windows-x64-setup.exe` — 64-bit Windows with WebView2.
- **Windows portable:** `SideB-windows-x64.zip` — extract and keep the executable, runtime DLLs and license together.
- SHA-256 checksum files are provided for both platforms.
