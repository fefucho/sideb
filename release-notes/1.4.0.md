# Side B 1.4.0

A new side player, a redesigned Home and Settings, and a smarter queue, together on macOS and Windows. Side B remains in beta.

## Now Playing side player

- A floating panel on the right with Lyrics, Now Playing and Queue, and the player controls pinned below. The bottom bar hands its controls to the panel with a reversible transition, and a round button outside the bar opens and closes it.
- Large, tall artwork with animated video when available, track details with Like and a menu, the next songs, and recommendations with a blurred cover background.
- An optional synced-lyrics preview at the bottom of the panel that opens the full lyrics.

## Queue and lyrics

- A continuous queue: previous songs fade above the current one, upcoming songs below, and recommendations in their own block.
- Drag previous or current songs to queue them again, and choose whether picking a recommendation plays only that song or advances up to it.
- LyricsPlus is tried first, and each song can use a lyrics source of your choice.

## Home, search and artists

- Home shows two continuous rails — Speed Dial and albums or playlists — with cards that keep their size when the window is resized, and a «See all» sheet.
- A new setting hides videos from search results and suggestions.
- Artist pages load faster with recycled shelves, and horizontal carousels keep their own scroll.

## Settings

- A larger Settings window with sections, monochrome icons and grouped lists, and a search field in the sidebar.

## Optional animated artwork from Spotify

- Spotify Canvas comes installed as a separate plugin on both platforms, **off by default**. It only appears in the side player, after Apple's artwork, and needs you to sign in to Spotify in Settings.
- It relies on unofficial Spotify web endpoints, so it may stop working at any time. You can remove the plugin from Settings without affecting anything else in Side B.
