# Side B 1.5.3

New side player layout and a native, resizable sidebar on macOS. Side B remains in beta.

## macOS

- **Native left sidebar:** the sidebar is now the system one. Drag its edge to make it wider or narrower (the width is remembered). Content scrolls flush under it, without a gap or fade on that side.
- **Resizable "Now Playing" panel:** drag its left edge to resize it between 340 and 520 pt; double-click the edge to reset it. Keyboard (arrow keys) and VoiceOver are supported.
- **Redesigned "Now Playing" panel:** the artwork uses the full panel width, vertical distances are even, and the background follows the album colors (and the video's, for portrait videos).
- **Content frame:** the page area is a rounded panel with a soft fade on its edges. A new "Content border" setting (Settings → Appearance) lets you choose None, Subtle or Strong.
- The top search and navigation controls now line up with the content area next to the sidebar.

## Spotify Jam

- **macOS gets the Windows fixes from 1.5.1 and 1.5.2:** joining a Jam with a short `spotify.link` link, and no more false "no equivalent in YouTube Music" for tracks whose titles have not arrived yet. A Jam track now takes the best YouTube Music match (use "choose another version" if it is wrong); "no equivalent" only appears when the search returns nothing.
- The Jam tab in full screen only appears while a Jam is active.
- Windows already had these fixes since 1.5.1 and 1.5.2.

## Settings

- **Exponential volume (macOS, optional):** a new setting in Settings that makes the volume slider finer at low levels. Off by default.

## Windows side player

- The "Now Playing" panel gets the same artwork width, even spacing and album-color background as macOS. This part has not been verified on WebView2 yet; the native sidebar, the content frame and the resizable panel are macOS-only for now.
