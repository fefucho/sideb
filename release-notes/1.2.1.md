# Side B 1.2.1

This patch updates search, navigation and playback controls on macOS and Windows. Side B remains in beta.

## macOS

- Redesigned top navigation with separate search, destination and Back/Forward glass capsules, improved spacing and smoother sidebar transitions.
- Search directly from the top bar with the same results and actions as quick Spotlight search. Use **Command K** for Spotlight or **Command F** for the top bar. Click outside to close it.
- Centered search text and restored Settings in the account and app menus.
- Fixed the two-finger gesture for lowering the fullscreen player: leaving hover during the animation no longer cancels a valid dismissal.
- Improved Home navigation and retained-page performance.

## Both platforms

- Fine volume adjustment by scrolling over the **open volume control** or the **front of the fullscreen artwork**. A temporary percentage appears on the artwork; the rest of fullscreen does not change volume.
- Improved search suggestions, spelling corrections, recent searches and personal-library filtering.
- Artist links for featured guests in track titles and more consistent playback controls.

## Windows

- Fixed active-track indicators and stale row selection when advancing through albums and playlists.
- Preserved source-row identity separately from queue ownership, including duplicate tracks and shuffle.
- Improved ordering of rapid volume and mute changes.

## Downloads

- **macOS:** `SideB-macOS.zip` — Apple Silicon, macOS 15 or later; ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID notarized.
- **Windows installer:** `SideB-Windows-x64-setup.exe` — 64-bit Windows with WebView2.
- **Windows portable:** `SideB-windows-x64.zip` — extract the whole archive and keep the executable, libmpv, Vulkan runtime and license together.
- SHA-256 checksums are included for both platforms.

Version **1.2.1**, public build **14**. Both packages are built and tested; their source revisions and verification details are recorded in `SideB-build-info.json`. Automated verification does not replace physical trackpad, audio, account or installer acceptance testing.
