# Side B 1.5.1

Small fix for Spotify Jam on Windows. Side B remains in beta.

## Spotify Jam

- **Short invitation links:** pasting a `spotify.link` invitation (the link the Spotify app produces when you share a Jam) now joins the Jam. Side B resolves the short link anonymously and joins with the regular `open.spotify.com/socialsession/…` link. If it cannot be resolved, paste the full link instead.

## Downloads

- **macOS:** `SideB-macOS.zip` — Apple Silicon, macOS 15 or later; ad-hoc signed, not notarized.
- **Windows installer:** `SideB-Windows-x64-setup.exe` — 64-bit Windows with WebView2.
- **Windows portable:** `SideB-windows-x64.zip` — extract and keep the executable, runtime DLLs, the `provider` folder and license together.
- SHA-256 checksum files are provided for both platforms.
