# Side B 1.5.0

Spotify Jam support as a guest with live incoming invitations, plus quick search and category browsing on macOS and Windows. Side B remains in beta.

## Listen together with Spotify Jam

- **Connections & shared Spotify session:** a unified **Connections** entry replaces the AirPlay-only button across the player bar, lateral sidebar, and fullscreen view (showing AirPlay alongside Spotify Jam on macOS). Spotify login is shared once across Spotify Canvas and Spotify Jam.
- **Live Jam mirror & queue control:** join a Spotify Jam via link or incoming invitation; Side B snapshots and pauses your personal queue, matches each Jam track to YouTube Music, mirrors live playback position with drift correction, and forwards permitted playback and queue edits while keeping local recommendations, radio, shuffle, and repeat off until you leave.
- **Live incoming invitations:** when signed in to Spotify, Side B listens in the background for incoming Jam invitations, pops a native floating toast banner with 1-click **Join** / **Dismiss**, lists active invitations inside **Connections**, and shows a badge indicator on the Connections button.
- Unofficial Spotify web endpoints are used via the separable `spotify-jam` session plugin; they may stop working at any time.

## Search, navigation and player bar

- Category browsing, quick spotlight search improvements, playback count formatting, and player bar refinements across macOS and Windows.

## Downloads

- **macOS:** `SideB-macOS.zip` — Apple Silicon, macOS 15 or later; ad-hoc signed, not notarized.
- **Windows installer:** `SideB-Windows-x64-setup.exe` — 64-bit Windows with WebView2.
- **Windows portable:** `SideB-windows-x64.zip` — extract and keep the executable, runtime DLLs, the `provider` folder and license together.
- SHA-256 checksum files are provided for both platforms.
